# 22 · Settings (`/app/settings`)

**Goal:** Account, preferences and privacy controls. Business and customs details live on their own page (`20-business-profile.md`).

## Sections
- **Profile:** name, email, phone, password or sign-in method.
- **Language and display:** Arabic or English, number format, date format (Hijri and/or Gregorian), currency display.
- **Notifications:** per event (quote ready, payment received, sample update, shipped, delivered, delay, problem) and per channel (email, in-app); other channels later.
- **Privacy and data:** download my data; delete my account `[TBD: policy and legal limits, especially for orders in progress]`.
- **Security:** active sessions, sign out everywhere, two-factor `[later]`.
- **Legal:** links to the terms, privacy, refunds, restricted products and IP drawer.

## Behaviour
- Say when a setting affects existing orders.
- Changing the email or password needs re-confirmation.
